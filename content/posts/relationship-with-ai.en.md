I've been thinking a lot about AI and related topics recently. After an emotionally draining first half of term, a second wave of emotional responses hit hard when I was talking to my father about 3rd year course choices and specialisation and he started pivoting me towards a pathway seemingly to be associated with AI. The acronym itself feels vague and could carry varying degrees of meaning to individuals. To me, the idea of being so close to it in terms of professional specialisation has thrown my mind instantly down a deep hole, feelings of despair and loss of direction overtook me. I felt trapped. Trapped by the choice between two options that seemed to signify much more about my personal values. It's the decision between passion and money, it seemed. 

In hindsight, fear and anxiety were a result of a lack of understanding, of the subject matter, which were accumulated over time. It would be interesting to compile what "AI" entails for me personally, before and now. 

Since I initially started gathering my thoughts, a few things have happened. I've landed a summer project quite directly to do with AI; I've subscribed to a paid version of AI and successfully reached maximum usage for my account type a few times; and quite frankly to my surprise, I've been roasted by AI for the first time and it was difficult to hear, despite the correct motivation. I feel like increased exposure has helped solify the idea that AI is indeed rapidly reshaping and reconstructing our reality and the only way to keep up is to learn it, use it and allow ourselves to reimagine the possibilities. The AI mindset is honestly quite similar to the nature of Engineering: you've just got to "make it work". One doesn't need to understand every single detail, but needs to be capable of a precise and compact prompt to initialise any task, and understand a vague idea of the process to fine tune the results. I've been personally amazed by times when the AI produced such high quality and thought provoking results, that it all felt like it was too good to be true.
