Got up at 4 am, running across field back and forth trying to put the last few items into trunk room 7. There simply wasn't enough time. Bolts charged waiting fees because I was 10 minutes late technically. It was actually right at the edge this time. I made it onto the coach, made it through the airport. The flight seemed to take forever, maybe because I was super tired but couldn't sleep properly on the plane. I didn't like Stansted airport, but the strawberry & pistachio mascarpone fig crostini at departure was so good, though it made me a bit sick because of the thick cream (the main issue was prob not eating then eating lots suddenly). 

All this happened before my usual waking time though. Porto airport was lovely, as in the people were nice. Everything was so good until I got on a taxi, the reason being I was really tired and it was raining so I wanted to be dropped right at my accommodation, but the bolts/uber pick up seemed to be further away where as taxi was right at the door. I checked the prices and thought it couldn't be that different and I was willing to pay a bit more for a closer ride. However, I was scammed really bad. The trip costed 37 euros (12 euros on bolts), and during which I also found the driver taking a longer route. I was only glad he didn't kidnap me or sell me off. I felt really sad and my family was raging, apparently towards the driver for scamming me; I also cried because I didn't believe in anyone anymore. The driver appearing nice made everything worse, as thinking back those questions were just to make sure I was unable to defend myself when it came to checking out. He asked for cash, so I gave cash to him. On top of that, he didn't even drop me off at my accommodation, he stopped at an earlier road, asking me to walk there, but it was so difficult due to the pebble streets and my really heavy suitcase and bags. 

In the afternoon, I had some food at an anime ramen place - gyoza. I wanted a snack like food as it was getting late and I wanted a proper dinner, so it was good deal. I also had a Portuguese tart and a drink called ___. Both were worth it I think. It was raining on and off. I went to the Clerigos Tower walked around to the bookshop (long queue and so crowded) and spent ages trying to find my 'perfect' book. I am happy with my purchase - Messages. I was hesitating between this and Orlando by Virginia Woolf. After that, I still had time so decided to push further to visit the Photography Museum, which turned out to be right next to Clerigos Tower. Super interesting, but I was so tired, so didn't have energy to read everything. I decided to stop there and walk back to accommodation to rest for a bit before dinner (booked for 7:30). The walking is all really close, but it's rainy and the roads are all sloped so it feels like some effort. Looking forward to dinner, this is a restaurant I reserved and am super excited for it.
