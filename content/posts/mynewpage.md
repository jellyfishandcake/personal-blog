I've been thinking a lot about AI and related topics recently. After an emotionally draining first half of term, a second wave of emotional responses hit hard when I was talking to my father about 3rd year course choices and specialisation and he started pivoting me towards a pathway seemingly to be associated with AI. The acronym itself feels vague and carries varying degrees of meaning to individuals. To me, the idea of being so close to it in terms of professional specialisation has thrown my mind instantly down a deep hole, feelings of despair and loss of direction overtook me. I felt trapped. Trapped by the choice between two options that seemed to signify much more about my personal values. It's the decision between passion and money, it seemed. 

In hindsight, fear and anxiety were a result of a lack of understanding, of the subject matter, which were accumulated over time. It would be interesting to compile what "AI" entails for me personally, before and now.
