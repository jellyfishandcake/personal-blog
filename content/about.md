Hello, this is Julia! I am a passionate engineer who loves to think, analytically and philosophically. I hope you will find interesting thoughts here as you follow my journey. 

## Problem statement
The purpose of writing to inform, persuade, shape and construct our world is powerful. While I spend most of time in the world of maths and logic, I realise the importance of one's ability to transform concepts into meaningful and accessible words. 

I really like the idea of "food for thoughts" and I would like to produce something that perhaps provides equivalent value for you my reader. 

It is important to make clear who the audiences of my writing are, who will find the most value in these words. At an early stage, I would like to make these for myself. I welcome those who follow along my journey, but it should be noted that I will be writing for my own benefits of developing writing and critical thinking skills, recording my own reflections on "food for thoughts" and encouraging a regular, healthy output of my voices in their own shape or form. 

As I write, I note two things: 1) the struggle to make meaning clear on the first attempt, not to mention the succinctness of my expressions 2) the uncertainty in frequencies of posts and whether updates should be made on the same page or a new page each time for documentation purposes. 

But, I shall remind myself, nothing is or will be perfect. Professional journalism is a result of dedicated practice and a repeated editing process, which I appreciate but shall not constrain my vision to. What I truly hope, to conclude, is being able to find my own voice through frequent thinking, refining and writing, which has benefits in so many aspects.
